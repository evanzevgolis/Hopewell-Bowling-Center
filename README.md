# Hopewell-Bowling-Center
This is a site for the Hopewell Bowling Center in Hopewell, VA, located at 115 South 15th Ave, Hopewell, VA. 23860. A nostalgic community bowling center run by the family of Michael Zevgolis for over 70 years. Here at the Hopewell Bowling Center, we offer a safe and fun experience at a low price for all families within the community.
